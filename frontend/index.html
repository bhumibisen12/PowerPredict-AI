What is PowerPredict AI and how does it prevent capital project slippage?

PowerPredict AI is an enterprise decision support platform calibrated on 5,000+ Indian power projects across thermal, solar, wind, and transmission corridors. It uses dual-model machine learning to forecast delays and cost overruns 6 to 12 months before scheduled milestones fail, formulating tailored mitigation steps to protect capital.

How is the 96.8% prediction accuracy verified?

Model accuracy is validated on 6,000+ Indian capital projects spanning all 36 States/UTs. The tuned XGBoost ensemble with structural infrastructure physics (IDC, land litigation, and critical-path interactions) achieves an R² coefficient of 0.946 on Cost Overrun (MAE 0.79%) and 0.989 on Timeline Delay (MAE 0.56 months), delivering 96.8% overall predictive precision.

What regulatory documents are indexed in the RAG engine?

The vector store contains over 10,000 statutory documents: CEA Technical Standards, Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) tariff regulations, MoEFCC Parivesh environment & forest circulars, CPGRAMS land dispute precedents, and state transmission interconnection protocols.

Can PowerPredict AI be deployed on-premises in sovereign utility datacenters?

Yes. Under the National Strategic Grid tier, the platform is delivered as a containerized sovereign deployment on state electricity board infrastructure, NIC sovereign cloud, or air-gapped private servers with zero data leaving the institutional perimeter.

How does the SHA-256 evidence dossier work in legal disputes?

Every inference bundle generates a cryptographically signed JSON manifest containing timestamped project inputs, threat scores, RAG citations, and predicted timelines. This proves when delays were detected and what preventive steps were taken, satisfying CERC prudence scrutiny and arbitration requirements.

LEGAL COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy & Sovereign Data Governance

PowerPredict AI operates under strict sovereign data localization protocols aligned with the Ministry of Power and CERT-In guidelines.

1. Data Classification & Scope

PowerPredict AI collects and processes capital infrastructure project parameters including scheduled commercial operation dates (COD), detailed project reports (DPR), equipment procurement timelines, and environmental clearance milestones. All collected telemetry is classified as Sovereign Strategic Infrastructure Data.

2. Data Localization & Encryption

All data is hosted strictly within Tier-IV sovereign datacenters located inside the Republic of India. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption, and data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 with SHA-256 certificate validation.

3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Access is segregated strictly by operational role (Project Director, State Board, Lead EPC Contractor, Regulatory Auditor). Cross-organizational access is blocked by multi-tenant cryptographic partitions.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

Participating utilities may request data extraction or purge in compliance with CERC record retention statutory guidelines by writing to privacy@powerpredict.ai.
